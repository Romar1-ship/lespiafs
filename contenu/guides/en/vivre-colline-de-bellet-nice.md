Bellet is a district of Nice. Bellet hill lies west of the city, above the Var valley.

You are coming for a few months, for an assignment, a training course or a job. Here is what daily life looks like on the hill. The rental contract, the *bail mobilité*, a French furnished lease of 1 to 10 months, is explained in [the complete guide to the bail mobilité](/en/guides/bail-mobilite-nice-complete-guide).

## Where is Bellet hill?

Bellet is part of the city of Nice. Its slopes overlook the left bank of the Var, north-west of the city centre.

The Les Piafs des Collines apartment is on Bellet hill. It is neither by the sea nor in the city centre. The hill is quiet without being isolated: cars do pass on the road, far from the bustle of the city centre. The beach is 7 minutes away by car, outside traffic.

From its two terraces, the view opens onto the Baie des Anges (Bay of Angels).

## What is Bellet wine?

Bellet is an appellation d'origine contrôlée (AOC). It was recognised by a decree of 11 November 1941. The wine is harvested only within the city of Nice.

A few facts about the appellation:

- reds and rosés made from folle noire and braquet;
- whites made from rolle, also called vermentino.

You live in the middle of a vineyard, without leaving the city of Nice.

## How do you visit the Bellet estates?

In 2026, a Bellet wine shuttle ran from June to September from the Magnan stop. It ran on Saturdays in June and September, and on Fridays and Saturdays in July and August. According to Explore Nice Côte d'Azur, a bus came about every 1 h 20, between 10:20 and 18:15.

It served seven partner estates:

- Château de Bellet;
- Domaine de la Source;
- Domaine de Vinceline;
- Collet de Bovis;
- Château de Crémat;
- Clos Saint-Vincent;
- Domaine Saint-Jean.

Check the next season's timetable with the tourist office. Ask each estate about its visiting and tasting days.

## How do you get around from Bellet hill without a car?

Bus 60 stops 50 m from the apartment. It connects with tram line 2 at Magnan.

According to Nice Côte d'Azur airport, line 2 links the airport terminals to the city centre in under 30 minutes. It serves the Jean Médecin and Port Lympia stops.

## How long by car from Bellet?

A private parking space is included, within the villa's enclosed grounds. Outside traffic, from the apartment:

- the beach: 7 min;
- airport: 20 min;
- Monaco: 22 min;
- Sophia Antipolis: 28 min.

## Where do you shop near Bellet?

The Nice Lingostière shopping centre is at 606 boulevard du Mercantour, 06200 Nice. According to the Côte d'Azur France tourist office, the shopping arcade is open from 9:00 to 20:00, Monday to Saturday. The hypermarket opens from 8:00 to 21:00, and on Sundays from 8:00 to 12:30.

Check the opening hours before you go: they may change.

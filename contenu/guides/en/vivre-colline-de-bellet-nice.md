Bellet is a district of Nice. It lies on the hills west of the city, above the Var valley.

You are coming for a few months, for an assignment, a training course or a job. Here is what daily life looks like on these hills, in a Nice apartment for 1 to 10 months.

## Where is Bellet hill?

Bellet is part of the city of Nice. Its slopes overlook the left bank of the Var, north-west of the city centre.

The Les Piafs des Collines apartment is on Bellet hill, west of Nice. From its two terraces, the view opens onto the Baie des Anges (Bay of Angels).

## What is Bellet wine?

Bellet is an appellation d'origine contrôlée (AOC). It was recognised by a decree of 11 November 1941. The wine is harvested only within the city of Nice.

A few facts about the appellation:

- reds and rosés made from folle noire and braquet;
- whites made from rolle, also called vermentino.

You live in the middle of a vineyard, without leaving the city of Nice.

## How do you visit the Bellet estates?

In 2026, a Bellet wine shuttle ran from June to September from the Magnan stop, on Saturdays in June and September, and on Fridays and Saturdays in July and August. According to Explore Nice Côte d'Azur, a bus came about every 1 h 20, between 10:20 and 18:15.

It served seven partner estates:

- Château de Bellet;
- Domaine de la Source;
- Domaine de Vinceline;
- Collet de Bovis;
- Château de Crémat;
- Clos Saint-Vincent;
- Domaine Saint-Jean.

Check the next season's timetable with the tourist office. Ask each estate about its visiting and tasting days.

## How do you get around from Bellet without a car?

Bus 60 stops 50 m from the apartment. It connects with tram line 2 at Magnan.

According to Nice Côte d'Azur airport, line 2 links the airport terminals to the city centre in under 30 minutes. It serves the Jean Médecin and Port Lympia stops.

## Which contract for a few months in Bellet?

**You pay the rent.** You sign a *bail mobilité*, a French furnished lease of 1 to 10 months, provided for by articles 25-12 et suivants de la loi n° 89-462 du 6 juillet 1989, introduced by the loi ELAN du 23 novembre 2018. It lasts 1 to 10 months, is neither renewable nor automatically extended, and the landlord cannot ask for a security deposit.

You must show a reason before signing: professional training, higher education, apprenticeship contract, internship, service civique, professional transfer or temporary assignment as part of your professional activity.

**Your employer pays.** A company, a relocation agency, an insurer or a production rents in its own name, from 3 months. The invoice is in its name, with no proof of reason.

The guarantee takes the form of a guarantor: your employer or a private individual. Visale is not available at this rent level.

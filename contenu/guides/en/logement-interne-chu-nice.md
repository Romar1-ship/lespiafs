Your posting is in Nice. You have a few weeks to find housing for one semester, sometimes two.

This guide is for medical interns, junior doctors, fellows and doctors in training. The suitable contract, the *bail mobilité*, a French furnished lease of 1 to 10 months, is explained in [the complete guide to the bail mobilité](/en/guides/bail-mobilite-nice-complete-guide). Here, we focus on your schedule.

## When do residency semesters start in Nice?

Nice comes under the ARS Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur. According to the ARS, the winter placement runs from November to April, and the summer placement from May to October.

The 2026-2027 winter semester starts in November 2026. The next semester change is set for 3 May 2027.

## Can one lease cover two residency semesters?

A semester lasts 6 months. It fits within a bail mobilité, which runs from 1 to 10 months.

Two consecutive semesters, from November to October, make 12 months. The bail mobilité covers 10 months at most. Beyond that, a new lease on the same home falls under the standard furnished lease.

For a funded fellowship, the institution, the foundation or the laboratory can rent in its own name, from 3 months.

## Where are CHU Pasteur 2 and the Centre Antoine Lacassagne?

The Pasteur 2 hospital of the CHU de Nice is at 30 voie Romaine, Nice. Tram line 1 serves it, at the Hôpital Pasteur stop.

The Centre Antoine Lacassagne is a cancer treatment centre. It is at 33 avenue de Valombrose, Nice.

## How long does it take to get to the hospital from the home?

The Les Piafs des Collines apartment is on Bellet hill, west of Nice. By car, outside traffic:

- Clinique Kantys Centre: 12 min;
- Valrose campus: 13 min;
- CHU Pasteur 2: 15 min;
- Centre Antoine Lacassagne: 16 min.

Without a car, bus 60 stops 50 m away. It connects with tram line 2 at Magnan.

## How do you get home after a night shift?

A private parking space within the grounds is included in the price. You come back from your shift and park at home.

Check-in is self-service: the access code is sent to you before your move-in date. The check-in inventory is done with the landlord at a time you agree together, on the day you start your post or the day before.

## When should you book housing for your residency?

As soon as your posting is known. Only one apartment is offered. Once rented, it is taken for the whole length of the lease.

For the May 2027 semester, your request can go out as soon as you have your posting, without waiting for 3 May.

Give your start date, the length of your placement and who pays the rent. Attach your posting or training certificate.

Request the date of your semester.

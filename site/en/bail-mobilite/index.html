Une force tranquille

Il y a des lieux que l'on choisit pour leur adresse. Celui-ci, on le choisit pour ce que l'on voit en sortant sur la terrasse.

Au premier étage d'une villa, sur la colline de Bellet, l'appartement regarde toute la Baie des Anges. Le matin, le soleil se lève sur la terrasse. La vue ne bouge pas, et c'est elle qui vous pose : une force tranquille, stable.

Le logement est traversant. La lumière entre des deux côtés, et le soir, le soleil se couche sur les collines. D'une terrasse à l'autre, on voit tout, de la mer jusqu'aux crêtes.

Et les jours de tempête, la mer se couvre de moutons jusqu'à l'horizon. Vous êtes à l'abri, en hauteur, et vous regardez la puissance des éléments comme peu de gens la voient.

Frequently asked questions

The essentials on the lease, the documents required and life in the apartment.

What is a bail mobilité?

It is a French furnished lease of 1 to 10 months, created by the ELAN law of 23 November 2018 (articles 25-12 et suivants de la loi du 6 juillet 1989). It can be neither renewed nor extended by tacit agreement, and the landlord may not ask for a security deposit.

Who can rent under a bail mobilité in Nice?

A person in professional training, higher education, an apprenticeship, an internship, civic service (service civique), a work relocation or a temporary assignment. Proof of the reason is requested before the lease is signed.

Can my employer pay the rent?

Yes. When the employer, a relocation agency or a production pays the rent, the lease is signed by the person living in the home, with the payer's letter of financial responsibility. The payer transfers the rent with the lease reference, and the rent receipt in the tenant's name states the payer.

What does the rent include?

The rent and the flat service-charge fee (water, electricity, air conditioning, fibre, parking, shared pool), and bed linen and towels provided with changes. A bottle of Bellet wine is waiting for you on arrival. The tenant receives no energy bill.

How much does it cost?

€3,650 per month with service charges, for every length: rent of €3,475 plus a flat service-charge fee of €175. On a 10-month lease, the last month's rent is free. This concession applies once per lease and does not change the lease rent.

Is the Visale guarantee accepted?

Visale, the French state rent guarantee, is not available at this rent level. The guarantee takes the form of a guarantor: your employer or a private individual.

Can the apartment be rented for a holiday?

No. Rented only under a French mobility lease (bail mobilité) of 1 to 10 months, to a tenant who can document a work assignment, a transfer, training, study or an internship. Not available for holidays, nightly stays or tourist rental.

Can I rent for less than a month?

No. The minimum stay is one month.

Can I rent for a month as an executive or a self-employed professional?

Yes, if you can document a professional reason: an assignment, a project, training. The mobility lease requires that document. The apartment is not rented without a reason.

Can I work from the apartment?

Yes. The second bedroom holds a work desk, and fibre broadband is installed. The living area and the terrace offer other places to set up.

What happens at the end of the lease?

A mobility lease does not renew. Its length can be amended once, up to 10 months in total. The tenant may leave at any time with one month's notice. Keys are returned to the landlord at the exit inventory.

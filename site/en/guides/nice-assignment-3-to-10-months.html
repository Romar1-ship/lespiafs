Your company is sending you to Nice for a few months. Or you are being transferred, and your family will join you later.

You have one goal: to be ready from your first day. This guide lists what to settle before you leave and when you arrive.

Key points
  • Bail mobilité from 1 to 10 months, or a lease in the company's name from 3 months.
  • One single invoice, all inclusive.
  • Invoice exempt from French VAT, in the payer's name.

01Which home for a 3 to 10 month assignment?

An unfurnished rental is in principle signed for 3 years when the landlord is a private individual (article 10 de la loi du 6 juillet 1989). A hotel is paid by the night. In between, two contracts are made for an assignment, both suited to a mid-term furnished rental in Nice.

The *bail mobilité*, a French furnished lease of 1 to 10 months, when you pay. It is provided for by articles 25-12 et suivants de la loi n° 89-462 du 6 juillet 1989, introduced by the loi ELAN du 23 novembre 2018. It covers 1 to 10 months, is neither renewable nor automatically extended, and the landlord cannot ask for a security deposit. Temporary assignment and professional transfer are among the reasons provided for. Proof is requested before signing.

The lease in the company's name, when the employer pays. Your company, a relocation agency or an insurer rents in its own name. At Les Piafs des Collines, this is possible from 3 months, with an invoice in its name, with no proof of reason.

02What documents should you prepare?

For a bail mobilité:

  • your assignment order, your transfer letter or your assignment contract;
  • an ID;
  • the guarantee: a guarantor, your employer or a private individual. Visale is not available at this rent level.

For a lease in the company's name: the contact details of the organisation that pays and the name of the occupant.

04What formalities when you arrive in Nice?

Your change of address. The online change-of-details service on service-public.fr notifies several bodies in one entry, including social security and pension funds. It can be used from 3 months before to 3 months after the move.

Home insurance. The tenant insures the home against tenant's risks. Ask your insurer for a certificate before moving in.

Energy and internet. Nothing to do. Electricity, water, air conditioning and fibre are already connected and included in the monthly price.

The keys. Check-in is self-service: the access code is sent to you before your move-in date. The check-in inventory and the furniture list are drawn up jointly with you when you move in.

05How do you get around Nice during an assignment?

By car. A private parking space is included, within the villa's enclosed grounds. From the apartment, outside traffic:

  • Clinique Kantys Centre: 12 min;
  • Valrose campus: 13 min;
  • CHU Pasteur 2: 15 min;
  • Centre Antoine Lacassagne: 16 min;
  • airport: 20 min;
  • Monaco: 22 min;
  • Sophia Antipolis: 28 min.

By bus and tram. Bus 60 stops 50 m away. It connects with tram line 2 at Magnan. According to Nice Côte d'Azur airport, line 2 links terminals 1 and 2 to the city centre in under 30 minutes, with a Jean Médecin stop and a Port Lympia stop.

By train to Monaco. According to SNCF TER Sud, 65 return trips a day link Nice and Monaco, in 20 minutes on average.

06Can you rent a scooter by the month in Nice?

Nice rental firms mostly list hourly, daily or weekly rates. For a month or more, ask the rental firm directly for a quote.

Check your licence before renting. According to service-public.fr, a B licence lets you ride a light 125 cc motorbike if you have held it for at least 2 years and have taken a 7-hour practical course.

The private parking space, within the villa's enclosed grounds, takes your two-wheeler.

Your posting is in Nice. You have a few weeks to find housing for one semester, sometimes two.

This guide is for medical interns, junior doctors, fellows and doctors in training. It explains how to find housing near CHU Nice, at CHU Pasteur 2 and the Centre Antoine Lacassagne, with a contract that fits the length of a placement: the *bail mobilité*, a French furnished lease of 1 to 10 months.

Key points
  • A 6-month rotation fits within a bail mobilité.
  • The lease can be in the institution's name from 3 months.
  • No security deposit under a bail mobilité.

01When do residency semesters start in Nice?

Nice comes under the ARS Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur. According to the ARS, the winter placement runs from November to April, and the summer placement from May to October. The 2026-2027 winter semester starts in November 2026, and the next semester change is set for 3 May 2027.

A semester lasts 6 months, a length within the 1 to 10 months of the bail mobilité.

02Where are CHU Pasteur 2 and the Centre Antoine Lacassagne?

The Pasteur 2 hospital of the CHU de Nice is at 30 voie Romaine, Nice. Tram line 1 serves it, at the Hôpital Pasteur stop.

The Centre Antoine Lacassagne is a cancer treatment centre. It is at 33 avenue de Valombrose, Nice.

04Which contract for an intern or a fellow?

The bail mobilité is made for you, as long as you show one of the reasons set by law. It is provided for by articles 25-12 et suivants de la loi n° 89-462 du 6 juillet 1989, introduced by the loi ELAN du 23 novembre 2018.

  • Length of 1 to 10 months, set at signing.
  • Neither renewable nor automatically extended.
  • No security deposit.
  • Open to people in professional training, higher education, an internship or a temporary assignment, among other reasons.

Your posting certificate or training certificate serves as proof of reason. It is requested before signing.

A 6-month semester fits in one lease. Two consecutive semesters, from November to October, make 12 months. The bail mobilité covers 10 months at most: beyond that, a new lease on the same home falls under the standard furnished lease.

05What if the hospital or the laboratory pays?

For a funded fellowship, the institution, the foundation or the laboratory can rent in its own name.

The lease is then in the organisation's name, from 3 months. The invoice is issued in its name. No proof of reason is required.

06What guarantee is needed?

Visale is not available at this rent level. The guarantee takes the form of a guarantor: your employer or a private individual.

08What is the apartment like?

77.51 m² on the 1st floor of a villa. Two bedrooms, of 13.91 and 12.88 m². Two shower rooms, one of them en suite with a walk-in shower, and a separate WC.

The second bedroom serves as a study to prepare your shifts, your staff meetings or your thesis. It can also host a partner or a visiting relative.

Two terraces of 10.58 and 15.75 m² overlook the Baie des Anges (Bay of Angels). A 4.95 m² laundry veranda holds a washing machine and a tumble dryer.

Heating and air conditioning run on a reversible heat pump. The energy rating (DPE) is class C for energy and A for climate. Fibre internet is installed.

A shared pool is open at set hours.

09How does arrival work after a journey or a shift?

Check-in is self-service: the access code is sent to you before your move-in date. The check-in inventory and the furniture list are drawn up jointly with you when you move in. You agree the time of this appointment with the landlord, on the day you start your post or the day before.

Bed linen and towels are provided on arrival. The kitchen has an induction island, an oven, a microwave, a dishwasher and a fridge-freezer.

10When should you book?

As soon as your posting is known. Only one apartment is offered: once rented, it is taken for the whole length of the lease.

Give your start date, the length of your placement and who pays the rent. Attach your posting or training certificate.

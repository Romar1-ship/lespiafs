Useful questions

Who can rent?

The bail mobilité is intended in particular for people on a temporary work assignment, relocating, in vocational training, in higher education, in an apprenticeship, on an internship or in civic service. The reason must be documented before signing.

For how long?

From 1 to 10 months. The bail mobilité can be neither renewed nor rolled over. Its length can be changed once by amendment, up to 10 months in total.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. The lease is still signed by the person living there. A letter of financial responsibility lets the employer pay. The rent receipt is in the tenant's name and states the payer.

Is there a security deposit?

No. The bail mobilité prohibits a security deposit. A guarantor may however be requested: guarantees are reviewed with the application.

What is at my expense?

Cleaning and home insurance are not included. The monthly price of €3,650 covers the rent and the stated service-charge fee.

Can I leave before the end?

Yes. You can leave at any time by giving the landlord one month's notice, by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt, by bailiff's deed or by hand delivery against a receipt. The rent and the service-charge fee remain due until the end of the notice period. No departure penalty is charged. Only the 10th-month rent discount, reserved for leases completed over their full 10 months, is not granted.

How does arrival work?

Keys are handed over and the inventory is done with the landlord.

Is the pool private?

No. It is shared, with set access times.

Can I rent for a holiday?

No. This offer is exclusively a bail mobilité rental, with proof of eligibility. It is not available by the night.
